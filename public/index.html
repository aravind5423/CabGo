FAQs

Frequently asked questions

We have given answers to the most popular questions below

01 How do I book a cab on CabGo?
+
Simply register or log in as a passenger, enter your pickup and destination locations, pick your preferred vehicle type (Hatchback, Sedan, SUV, Auto, or Bike), and click "Confirm & Request Ride". The system instantly connects you with nearby drivers.
02 Which payment methods are supported?
-
We support a wide range of payment methods including Cash paid directly to the driver upon trip completion, Credit/Debit cards, UPI payments, and the built-in CabGo digital wallet balance. You can select your preferred payment option when booking.
03 How does upfront fare calculation work?
+
Fares are dynamically calculated using fixed base rates and per-kilometer distance rates defined by system administrators. You see the transparent estimate before confirming, so there are no unexpected surprises at the end of your trip.
04 How can I register as a driver?
+
Head over to the Registration page and select the "Driver" tab. Submit your valid commercial driver's license number, license expiry date, and vehicle details. Once vetted and approved by our administrators, you can go online and start accepting ride requests.
05 Can I cancel a ride after requesting?
+
Yes! If your ride request is still pending driver acceptance, you can cancel it instantly with one click and zero cancellation fee directly from the live tracking screen.
