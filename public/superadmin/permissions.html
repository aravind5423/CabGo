Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP) Notice

Every permission grants explicit execute rights to specific API endpoints and views in the operations console. Superadmin accounts inherently bypass permission filters with full platform governance. All grant and revoke events are written immediately to the immutable system_logs audit trail.
